Installing

  1. Open DevInspector.dmg and drag DevInspector into your Applications folder.
  2. Because DevInspector is a free app distributed outside the App Store, macOS may say it "is damaged" on first launch. This is just Gatekeeper — to allow it, open Terminal and run:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DevInspector.app
    Then open DevInspector normally.
